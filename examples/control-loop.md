# Feedback-loop stability

Phase margin measures how far a feedback loop is from the phase condition that would make negative feedback become positive feedback at unity loop gain. More phase margin generally indicates greater relative stability and less ringing, although the required margin depends on the system and its performance constraints.

## Review procedure

Plot or calculate loop gain, find the unity-gain crossover frequency, and measure the remaining phase before minus 180 degrees. Confirm the result across component tolerance, loading, temperature, and operating conditions.
